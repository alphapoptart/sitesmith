      Netlify DropNo account needed to start

      Netlify Drop takes a zip straight from your phone, and the contact form on the site starts working the moment it lands there.

      1. Tap Save the zip — it goes to Files.
      2. Open app.netlify.com/drop.
      3. Tap the drop area, choose Browse, and pick the zip.
